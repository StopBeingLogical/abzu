The **Empire of Branoch** is a sophisticated, high-concept worldbuilding and storytelling project, organized within an Obsidian vault. Based on the directory structure and internal documents, it appears to be a work-in-progress aimed at creating a cohesive setting for both **prose fiction** and **role-playing games** (with mentions of 5e and Pathfinder).

### **Core Philosophy & Themes**
The project is driven by a strong, cynical "moral engine." Rather than focusing on traditional fantasy tropes like "destiny" or "chosen ones," the universe is built around a specific human failure mode: **the confusion of delegation with abdication.** 

The central thesis is that atrocity occurs when individuals let systems, bureaucracies, or procedures make moral decisions for them to avoid personal responsibility. This theme permeates the history (from mage-dictators to the bureaucratic Council) and the character arcs (such as Senrew Anador and Phineas).

### **Project Structure**
The project is meticulously organized to facilitate a "distillation" workflow, moving information from raw notes to a refined canon:

1.  **`01_Refined_Canon`**: The "source of truth" for the world, containing organized information on Eras, Magic Ontology, and Meta-Governance.
2.  **`02_Derivative_Artifacts`**: The creative outputs of the setting, including **Prose** (stories/drafts), **RPG Campaigns**, and a specialized tool or system called the **Chimera Engine**.
3.  **`04_Atlas` & `05_Compendium`**: Detailed encyclopedic data covering geography (Regions, Settlements, Points of Interest) and lore (Characters, Factions, Artifacts).
4.  **`06_Mechanics`**: Likely contains the rules or systems for interacting with the world (e.g., for gaming).
5.  **`07_Inbound_Processing`**: A massive "working area" containing the raw material being processed. This includes:
    *   **`Raw_Corpus_Dumps/Prose`**: A vast collection of source documents, character outlines, and worldbuilding notes in various formats (.doc, .odt, .pdf).
    *   **`Raw_Corpus_Dumps/RPG_Corpus`**: Campaign notes, maps, and module drafts.
    *   **`Raw_Corpus_Dumps/Kindling`**: Early ideas and foundational materials.

### **Summary of Lore**
*   **The Transition of Power**: The world moved from a period of brutal, honest "mage-dictators" to a more stable, but insidious, Empire. This transition was achieved not by destroying old power, but by incorporating it into a bureaucratic system (The Council) that uses "peace" and "procedure" to mask the continuation of control and historical erasure.
*   **The Nageri**: Unrestricted beings whose magic was a natural part of existence, whose presence challenged centralized authority and contributed to the "Sundering."
*   **Narrative Rule**: The project explicitly rejects "destiny" or "prophecy." A story is considered part of this universe only if its inciting incident involves someone choosing not to decide, letting a system decide for them instead.
